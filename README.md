# Excel-Based-Sales-Performance-Customer-Analysis-Using-Excel

### **Overview**

This project involves analyzing coffee retail sales data and converting raw transactional records into an interactive business intelligence dashboard using Microsoft Excel. The primary objective was to assess sales performance and extract actionable insights related to revenue trends, customer behavior, product performance, and regional contribution.

Data cleaning and preparation were performed directly in Excel using built-in functions and structured transformations to ensure accuracy, consistency, and analytical readiness. Pivot Tables, calculated fields, charts, and slicers were then leveraged to enable dynamic analysis across time periods, customer segments, and product attributes.

The final deliverable is a consolidated, executive-ready Excel dashboard that presents key metrics and insights in a clear and intuitive format. The project highlights strong business understanding, attention to data quality, and hands-on proficiency in Excel for analytics and decision support.

### Project Objective

To analyze coffee sales performance across time, customers, products, and geographies, and convert transactional data into actionable business insights using Microsoft Excel.

### Dataset Overview


- Structure: Three related tables (Orders, Customers, Products) combined for analysis using Excel lookup functions
  Core Data Areas: Orders, Customers, Products

- Rows: 1000

- Key Fields: Order Date, Country, Coffee Type, Roast Type, Size, Quantity, Sales, Customer Name, Loyalty Status

**The tables were merged within Excel using XLOOKUP and related functions, followed by formula-based cleaning and standardization to ensure analytical accuracy.**



### Tools & Techniques Used

- Microsoft Excel

- Excel functions for data cleaning and standardization

- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts

- Slicers for interactive filtering

- Calculated fields for sales and performance analysis

### Dashboard Highlights

- Time-based sales trends with Year-over-Year and monthly analysis

- Identification of top customers based on revenue contribution

- Country-wise sales performance comparison

- Product-level performance by coffee type, roast type, and pack size

- Comparison of loyal versus non-loyal customer revenue contribution

### Key Insights

- December consistently generated the highest sales, showing strong Year-over-Year growth.

- A limited number of high-value customers contributed a significant share of total revenue.

- The United States emerged as the leading revenue-generating market.

- Medium and Large pack sizes outperformed smaller sizes in overall sales value.

- Loyal customers demonstrated higher repeat purchase value compared to non-loyal customers.

### Deliverable

An interactive Excel dashboard designed for quick interpretation of business performance and informed decision-making.

This project demonstrates practical Excel-based business intelligence capabilities, including sales analysis, customer segmentation, and product performance evaluation, making it relevant for roles in analytics, marketing, and business development.
